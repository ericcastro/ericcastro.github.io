# Blog writing

- Use only the ASCII hyphen-minus (`-`) for dashes in all blog post writing.
- Never use em dashes (U+2014) or en dashes (U+2013). This applies to post titles, body text, summaries, captions, frontmatter text, and related blog copy.
- When creating or editing a blog post, check the entire post and replace any em or en dashes with `-` before finishing.
